# My writing journey

I started writing when I was about 10 years old, creating short stories
for friends on special occasions. Those early works, however, remain
classified and are not available for public consumption.

It wasn't until college that I discovered technical writing — or realized
that writing could become a career in the software industry. That discovery
eventually led me from an English degree to a career documenting everything
from networking software and machine learning platforms to APIs, developer
integrations, and documentation systems.

Because I didn't know technical writing existed when I started college,
I've always enjoyed talking with English students about the different paths
an English degree can lead to and what it's like to work alongside engineers
and product teams in the software industry.

I still write a few short stories each year to keep a little creative writing
in my life. And during the early days of COVID, I even let my late dog Riley
start a [blog](blog-archived/riley-blog.md).

<h2 class="writing-journey__closing">Still writing</h2>

Whether I'm documenting an API, experimenting with a new tool, building a
website, or writing something just for fun, I've always been drawn to the
same basic challenge: taking an idea and figuring out the clearest way to
communicate it.
